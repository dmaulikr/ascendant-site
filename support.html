Need a hand?

Last updated 5 November 2026.

Write to dmaulikr@gmail.com with a question, a bug or an idea. If you're testing through TestFlight, you can also take a screenshot in the app and send it as feedback.

If you're not okay right now, tap "I'm not okay" — it's always within reach in the app. Everything stops, and Ascendant shows a crisis line for where you are and your emergency number. Away from the app, findahelpline.com lists free crisis lines by country. In danger right now? Call your local emergency number.

Beginning

The first time you open Ascendant, the System introduces itself and asks for one promise — press and hold to make the Vow. Then it asks what pulls at you hardest and how much of yourself you can give right now. That's all it needs to begin; it learns the rest as you climb. Your first directive is three slow breaths, before you rise tomorrow.

The day

  • Dawn — open Ascendant to receive the day. It hands you one to three directives, each in a window: morning, midday or evening.
  • A Climb — open a directive to begin it. Mark it done when it's done, or stop when you need to.
  • Not today — declines a window. It costs nothing, and it never counts against you.
  • Dusk — one question about how the day felt. "That was a lot" makes tomorrow lighter.
  • Rest — one day in seven is a rest day. Nothing is asked, and there's no call.

The call

Ascendant can call to you once a day, at dawn — at the hour you choose. Set it, or silence it, in Self → Your terms. It never calls on a rest day, it grows quieter when you're away, and it never calls while you've told it you're not okay. If no call arrives, check that notifications for Ascendant are on in the iPhone's Settings → Notifications.

Self, Codex and Path

  • Self shows what you are becoming: the four pillars, and what the System has noticed, if it has noticed anything yet. Touch a light to see what grows within it.
  • Codex holds the truths you've earned. The rest stay sealed until you reach them.
  • Path is the night sky of your climb: the ranks you've crossed, and the next one.

There are no scores and no numbers. Progress is shown in light.

"I'm not okay"

Say it, and everything stops — no directives, no call, nothing asked of you. The System shows people you can talk to where you are. The pause ends only when you say so, with "I'm ready to begin again"; the rest of that day stays quiet and the climb returns at dawn. Ascendant is not a crisis service, and it never guesses how you feel.

Your terms

Self → Your terms holds everything you set: the call and its hour, whether the System tells you what it notices, the sound of its voice, and your name. It's also where you erase your journey.

A new iPhone

Your journey lives on your iPhone and travels in your iPhone's own backup. Restore your new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup, and your climb comes with it. There's no account to sign in to.

How the directives are written

Every directive, and every word the System says, was written before the app shipped: drafted with the help of an AI model, checked line by line against the System's Oath, and chosen by hand. Nothing is generated on your iPhone, and nothing is sent anywhere to compose your day.

Starting over

Self → Your terms → Erase this journey deletes everything Ascendant keeps on your iPhone, and the climb begins again from the start. It can't be undone.
