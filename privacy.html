Privacy Policy

What we collect: nothing.

Last updated 5 November 2026.

Ascendant learns you so it can ask the right things of you. Everything it learns stays with you, on your iPhone. We never see it.

What we collect

Nothing. Ascendant does not collect, transmit, share or sell any personal information. There is no Ascendant server, no account, no analytics, no advertising identifier, no tracking and no crash reporting tied to you. The app makes no network requests of its own.

What Apple shows us

Ascendant adds no analytics of its own. Like every developer on the App Store, we can see the totals Apple reports: how many times the app's page was viewed and the app downloaded and, only from people who chose to share analytics with app developers, totals such as how many devices opened it, how many came back a day, a week or four weeks later, and crash reports. These are counts of everyone together. Apple doesn't show us who you are, and we never ask. You can stop sharing in your iPhone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements → Share With App Developers.

What stays on your iPhone

To learn you, Ascendant keeps a record on your iPhone, in storage private to the app:

  • your answers when you begin (what pulls at you, how much room you have) and the name you give, if you give one;
  • the directives you're given, and what you do with them — held, set down, "Not today" — and the time you spend in a Climb;
  • your answer at dusk about how the day felt;
  • your choices in "Your terms": the call and its hour, whether it tells you what it notices, and its voice.

From this record the app works out, on your iPhone, what to ask of you next. None of it is sent anywhere.

Your backup

Like any app's data, your journey is included in your iPhone's own backup — iCloud Backup or a backup on your computer — if you have backups turned on. That backup belongs to you and your Apple ID; we cannot see or reach it. It is how your journey moves with you to a new iPhone.

The call

If you allow notifications, Ascendant's daily call is scheduled on your iPhone by iOS. Nothing is sent from a server. You can change its hour or silence it in "Your terms", or turn notifications off in the iPhone's Settings.

"I'm not okay"

When you tell Ascendant you're not okay, it stops asking anything of you and shows ways to reach people: a crisis line for your region, an international directory of help lines (findahelpline.com), and your emergency number. The region is read from your iPhone's own settings, on the device. Ascendant never contacts anyone. A line opens your phone or your browser only when you tap it, and what you say there is between you and them.

Health data

Ascendant does not read from or write to Apple Health, and uses no sensors. It knows only what you tell it and what you do in the app.

Erasing your journey

Self → Your terms → Erase this journey deletes everything Ascendant keeps on your iPhone. Deleting the app does the same. A backup made earlier keeps its copy until that backup is replaced or deleted.

Feedback

If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write, and use them only to reply and to improve Ascendant.

The first climbers

If you write to us to join the first climbers, we keep your email address only to tell you when the doors open. We don't add it to any service, and we delete it within 30 days of the App Store launch, or sooner if you ask.

Testing with TestFlight

If you test Ascendant through Apple's TestFlight, Apple shares with us any feedback and screenshots you choose to send, crash reports, and — if you were invited by email — the name and email address you were invited with. We use them only to run the test, and we delete our notes within 30 days of the test ending. The app itself still sends nothing.

Third parties

Ascendant includes no third-party SDKs, trackers or advertising. It uses only Apple's own frameworks.

This website

These pages are served by GitHub Pages. As it does for every Pages site, GitHub logs a visitor's IP address for security; we can't see those logs. The pages carry their own fonts and ask no other server for anything. There are no cookies and no analytics.

Children

Because Ascendant collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. If you are under 18 and want to write to us, please do it with a parent or guardian.

Your rights

If you have written to us — feedback, the first climbers, or through TestFlight — you can ask at any time what we hold, ask us to correct or delete it, or withdraw your consent. Write to dmaulikr@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date before the change ships.
