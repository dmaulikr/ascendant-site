One full day

Ascendant's first directive comes at the next dawn, so on day one the app ends on "Your first day begins at dawn." So you needn't wait, this is that first day and the next, from a fresh install: 12 minutes.

No sound. Download the MP4 (25 MB)

What it shows

  • 0:17Day one, 19:45. Onboarding: the Vow, what pulls at you, a name, how much you can give, the first directive, and the call. It ends on "Your first day begins at dawn."
  • 4:37The next morning, 07:30. Dawn Sync gives the day. The Channel's two windows. The morning Climb, ended with "It's done".
  • 6:14The same day, 18:30. The evening Climb, then the Reckoning at dusk, answered. "Until dawn."
  • 8:01The rest of the app. Self, Self → Your terms (the call, its hour, sound, name and erase), the Codex and Path.
  • 11:02"I'm not okay". The day stops and real people to reach are shown; "I'm ready to begin again" ends it.

How it was made

On the iOS Simulator (iPhone 18 Pro, iOS 27.0), from a debug build of the code in version 1.0.0 (9). Every tap is made in the app as it ships, from a fresh install; nothing is seeded.

  • The app's clock is moved forward between scenes, with a launch setting that only debug builds have; the build under review has no such setting.
  • The status bar is set to show the app's time.
  • The cuts are only between scenes, and each scene starts once the app has opened. Nothing within a scene is cut or sped up.
  • The help lines shown are for the simulator's region, India. A phone in the United States shows the 988 Lifeline and 911.

No sign-in and no network: everything stays on the device. Questions: dmaulikr@gmail.com.
