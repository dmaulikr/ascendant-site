Ascendant: Life RPG · for iPhone

I am what you are becoming.

There you are. The connection is faint across time, and sharpens as you climb.

Ascendant is a quiet System that speaks as the self you are becoming. It learns you one day at a time — what you hold, what you set down, how the day felt — and asks only what you can give. Mind, Heart, Bond and Form: four pillars, one climb.

A day, in three movements

  • Dawn. One call, at the hour you choose. The day's directives wait for you.
  • The windows. Morning, midday, evening — a few small things to do, sized to you. "Not today" costs nothing.
  • Dusk. One honest question about how it felt. Tomorrow is shaped by the answer.

It earns the right to lead

At first it suggests. Only as it comes to know you does it begin to direct — and it never claims to know more than it does. No streaks, no scores, no shame. Rest comes one day in seven, and rest is sacred.

When it isn't a good day

Say "I'm not okay", and everything stops. The System steps back and points you to people who can help, where you are. It never guesses at how you feel; it listens only to what you tell it.

Nothing leaves your phone

No account, no server, no analytics, no advertising. Your journey lives on your iPhone and in your own backup. Read the privacy policy.

The first climbers

Ascendant is in private testing now. If you'd like to be among the first to climb, write a line and you'll hear when the doors open — once, and never more than that.

I want to climb

Ascendant is a companion for small, steady growth. It is not medical care, therapy or a crisis service.
